About Kirya

The friendly way to rent a car

Kirya connects travelers and locals with trusted car-rental vendors across Morocco — and beyond. We make renting effortless: browse, compare, book, and drive.

Verified fleet

Every car and vendor is reviewed. Real photos, real specs, real availability in real time.

Book in seconds

Pick your dates, confirm, and you're set. Secure payment with cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Everywhere you go

From Casablanca to Marrakech, find the right car in the right place — with local support.

Transparent pricing

No surprises. See the full price up front, in MAD or EUR, before you confirm.

Travel eSIMs too

Stay connected on the road. Buy a data eSIM right inside the app and install it instantly.

Support that cares

In-app chat and a team that actually responds — in your language.
